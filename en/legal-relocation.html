03 Service

Legal relocation

Moving to Panama opens several legal fronts at once: your immigration status, perhaps a work permit, a lease or a purchase, and sometimes a company. We help you organise them into a single plan, in the right order, so one step does not hold up the next.

Who it is for
Individuals and families planning to settle in Panama

Including those relocating a professional or business activity.

What is included

The legal side of your move

We coordinate the legal work. Logistics are decided by you and your providers.

01

Pre-move document plan

Which documents to obtain, apostille or translate in your country before you travel, and in what order, so you do not have to repeat steps from abroad.

02

Residency and work permit

Coordination of the immigration component and, where relevant, the employment one, within the same plan. Details under Residency and Work permits.

03

Lease review

Legal review of the lease before you sign: term, deposits, grounds for termination and each party’s obligations.

04

Guidance on buying property

Title search and contract review before you commit. Details under Contracts, companies & real estate.

05

Guidance on setting up a company

Which structure is worth considering and what legal steps it involves, in coordination with your accountant.

06

Schedule of steps and appointments

Organisation of the legal steps and of the appointments that require your presence, grouped together where possible.

Not part of this service

House hunting, removals, transport, customs clearance of household goods, school enrolment and opening bank accounts. We can review the legal documents those providers ask you for, but we do not provide those services or guarantee their outcome. Account approval is solely the bank’s decision.

How we work

One plan, in stages

1

Diagnosis

What needs to be resolved before you arrive, on arrival and afterwards. We check for conflicts of interest and tell you what we can take on.

2

Written proposal

Components included, the fee for each and third-party costs listed separately: government fees, apostilles, translations and notary.

3

Preparation from your country

Documents, contracts and applications are prepared remotely, with your review.

4

Execution in Panama

Filings, signings and appointments on the agreed schedule, with follow-up until each component is closed.

Some steps will be in person

This service coordinates several procedures and some require your presence in Panama. We give you a schedule of the expected appointments so you can plan for them.

For the initial assessment

What to have ready

For guidance only. To prepare the diagnosis it helps to know:

  • Your current country of residence and the nationalities of everyone moving.
  • Approximate moving date.
  • Whether you will work in Panama, invest, retire or relocate a business.
  • Whether you plan to rent or buy a home.
  • Which documents you already have apostilled or translated.
Please do not send documents through the form

We will tell you in writing which documents to prepare and how to send them.

Frequently asked questions

Worth knowing before you start

Will you find me a home or organise the move?

No. Our service is the legal component. We can review the lease or purchase contract you choose, but house hunting and logistics are outside it.

Can I start before travelling?

Yes, and it is advisable. Many documents are easier to obtain and apostille in your home country than from abroad once you are in Panama.

Can you open a bank account for me?

No. Account opening depends on each bank’s policies. We can tell you which legal documents banks usually ask for, without guaranteeing approval.

Does this service include residency?

It coordinates residency as part of the plan. Which components your case includes, and the cost of each, are set out in the written proposal.

Planning your move to Panama?

Tell us when and with whom you plan to settle. We will propose a legal plan in stages.
