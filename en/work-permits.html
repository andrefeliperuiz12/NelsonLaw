02 Service

Work permits

To work in Panama, a foreign national needs an authorisation that does not always come with residency. We help you identify the permit that applies, gather the worker’s and the company’s documents, and keep track of expiry dates.

Who it is for
Foreign workers and the companies that hire them

If the worker and the employer both contact us about the same application, we agree at the outset whom we represent.

What is included

The authorisation and its paperwork

The service covers permission to work and the hiring documents.

01

Which permit applies

We review the worker’s profile, immigration status and role, and tell you which permit applies and what conditions it carries.

02

Applications and renewals

Preparation and filing of the application with the Ministry of Labour (MITRADEL), and renewals before expiry.

03

Worker and employer documents

Review of the worker’s personal documents and of the company documents the procedure requires, to avoid preventable objections.

04

Employment contract for the hire

Drafting or review of the employment contract filed with the application, consistent with the permit and with labour law.

05

Follow-up and expiry dates

Follow-up until a decision is issued, and a calendar of expiry dates for the permit and related documents.

Not part of this service

Employment litigation, dismissal claims and payroll management. We do not guarantee approval of the permit or its timing either.

How we work

How we handle the application

1

Initial assessment

We review your enquiry, check for conflicts of interest and tell you whether we can take the matter on, what deadlines apply and what is needed.

2

Written proposal

Scope, fees and third-party costs listed separately, plus what is not included. Nothing starts until you accept it.

3

Remote preparation

Documents, filings and strategy are handled by email, video call or messaging, and you review them before anything is filed.

4

Filing and follow-up

Filing with the relevant authority, responses to its requests and progress updates until a decision is issued.

Filing channel and in-person steps

Depending on the type of permit, the application may be filed online or in person, and some stages may require the worker or a company representative to attend. The available channel is confirmed for each application during the assessment.

For the initial assessment

What to have ready

For guidance only. To start, these details are enough:

  • The worker’s nationality and, if already in Panama, their immigration status.
  • Role, duties and expected start date.
  • General details of the hiring company.
  • For a renewal, the expiry date of the current permit.
  • The contract or offer letter, if one exists.
Please do not send documents through the form

The final list depends on the permit and is provided in writing after the assessment.

Frequently asked questions

Worth knowing before you start

Are residency and a work permit the same thing?

No. Residency allows you to stay in the country; a work permit allows you to work. Some residency categories are linked to employment and others do not include permission to work.

We review this case by case. See also Residency & immigration.

Who applies, the worker or the company?

It depends on the type of permit. Often both are involved and the company provides its own documents. We tell you in the assessment.

What happens if the permit expires?

Working on an expired permit can have consequences for both the worker and the employer. That is why we track expiry dates and prepare renewals in advance.

Do you handle employment disputes or dismissals?

No. This service covers permission to work and the hiring paperwork. Employment litigation is not part of our services.

Can you handle my matter in English?

Yes. We handle enquiries in English and Spanish, so you can write to us in whichever you prefer.

Proceedings before Panamanian authorities are conducted in Spanish, and foreign documents usually need an official translation. During the assessment we tell you which documents need translating.

Describe the case in a few lines and we will tell you which permit to assess and which documents to gather.
